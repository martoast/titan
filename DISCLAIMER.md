# Titan — Disclaimer & Risk Acknowledgment

**Read this before you build, flash, wear, or use anything from the Titan project.**

Titan is an **open-source, DIY wellness and quantified-self project**. It is a
reference design that you build for yourself. It is **not a product, it is not sold
as a finished device, and it is not a medical device.**

---

## 1. Not a medical device — wellness only

> **Titan is not a medical device. It is for general wellness and informational
> purposes only and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent
> any disease. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult
> a qualified healthcare professional before changing your health regimen.
> Measurements have limitations and are not guaranteed accurate. Any action you take
> is at your own risk.**

Titan reports general-wellness metrics only — heart rate, heart-rate variability,
sleep, skin temperature, activity, and derived stress/recovery/readiness signals.

Titan does **not** and **must not** be used to:

- detect, diagnose, or monitor any disease or medical condition;
- detect arrhythmia, atrial fibrillation (AFib), or perform ECG;
- detect or screen for sleep apnea;
- estimate or monitor blood pressure;
- measure clinical-grade blood oxygen (pulse oximetry) for any medical purpose.

If Titan ever tells you something about your body that worries you, **talk to a
doctor, not to the device.** The numbers can be wrong. They are not a diagnosis.

## 3. Safety

Building Titan involves a **lithium-polymer battery** and a device worn in
**prolonged contact with your skin**. Both carry real, physical risks. Before you
start, read [`opensource/SAFETY.md`](opensource/SAFETY.md) in full and follow it.
